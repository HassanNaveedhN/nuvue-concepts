Studio

Design that behaves like architecture, applied to how you live.

NUVUE Concept was founded on a simple belief: furniture and interiors should be reasoned about with the same rigor as a building — load, proportion, material honesty — then softened into something you actually want to live in. We work in small teams, close to the material, from first sketch to final install.

How a project moves

Four stages. No stage is skipped, regardless of scope.

01

Brief & site read

We walk the space, take measurements and listen for how it's actually used before drawing anything.

02

Concept & plan

Layout, material palette and furniture direction, presented as drawings you can react to.

03

Build & fabricate

Custom pieces are made in-studio; trades are coordinated against a single working drawing set.

04

Install & handover

Final placement, styling and a walkthrough of every material and finish used.
